# Is the database missing many plants? Open aggregate statistics and maker rankings (T062)
Status: unreviewed

## Summary (5 lines max)
- No open source gives a China cell-capacity total above ~3 TWh for 2025; the best figures are CRU ">2 TWh in 2024" (all Li-ion) and a Dec-2023 expert forecast of ~3 TWh for 2025 (not an actual). Our China 2.6-3.2 TWh (2025) is consistent with these; reported output is 1.10 TWh (2024) and 1.76 TWh (2025).
- Reported utilisation (~47% 2023, "generally <60%" 2025, unnamed sources) implies China capacity of roughly 2.9-3.8 TWh in 2025 for 1.76-1.88 TWh of output/shipments - same range as ours. Weak evidence: capacity definitions are not stated by any source.
- All CABIA 2025 TOP15 domestic installers are in the DB except Inpower (因湃, rank 12), Yuanhang Jinli (远航锦锂, rank 14) and Mengguli (ternary rank 12); ENERGEE (吉曜通行, rank 10) is partial (2 of 8-11 bases). Inpower is the only concrete lead (Panyu, Guangzhou; 18 GWh of lines built, search snippet only).
- No open aggregate for Japan/Korea domestic capacity found (METI 403, JETRO gives only the 150 GWh 2030s target); Europe/US not searched (budget). IEA pages returned HTTP 403 - IEA figures not obtained.
- Quotes below were extracted by the fetch tool's summariser, not read raw: run `checks/verify_quotes.py`-style re-fetch before relying on exact wording. Items marked "snippet (not opened)" come only from search-tool summaries.

## Conflicts between sources
- Capacity at different dates are not monotonic: CABIA 1,860 GWh power-battery capacity at end-June 2023 (T062-A-001) vs Volta-plus 1,500 GWh/yr at end-2023 (T062-A-002). Different definitions (built vs effective vs including pilot/idle); neither states which.
- 2024 output: CABIA 1,096.8 GWh (power + other, A-005) vs national output 1,170 GWh (Zhiyan) and GGII shipments 1,175 GWh (snippets, A-012).
- 2025 output: CABIA Dec-release 1,755.6 GWh (A-006) vs Jan-Nov 1,468.8 GWh + Dec 201.7 GWh = 1,670.5 GWh (snippet): ~85 GWh gap, probably a revision - not confirmed.
- "3000 GWh" is a Dec-2023 forecast for 2025 capacity (A-008) but is repeated in 2025-26 articles as "planning" or as scope "domestic" vs "global" within the same article (A-009).
- CABIA 2024 capacity ">2000 GWh built, output <900 GWh, utilisation <50%" (search-summary, unopened, source article not identified) vs CRU ">2 TWh" (A-004): consistent on capacity, but 900 GWh output is below CABIA's own 1,096.8 GWh (output definitions differ: power-only vs power + other).
- Utilisation: sector-average ~47-60% (A-003, A-009) vs CATL/EVE 90-97% (A-010, A-011): average is dominated by idle tier-2/3 capacity; this matters because tier-2/3 plants are the likeliest to be absent from DB.
- IEA figures (2.5 TWh 2023, >3 TWh 2024, >4 TWh 2025 per the task brief) could NOT be re-checked: IEA pages returned HTTP 403 and the GEVO 2026 PDF exceeded the fetch limit.
